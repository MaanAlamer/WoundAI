About WoundSense AI

A modern clinical AI platform for wound analysis, triage support, and follow-up review.

Our Mission

WoundSense AI helps care teams evaluate wound images with consistent visual outputs. The platform combines segmentation overlays and confidence-based classification to support triage workflows and trend-based monitoring.

Clinical Support

Highlights wound boundaries and provides infection likelihood in seconds.

Care Continuity

Keeps historical records to compare healing progress over time.

Responsible Use

Designed as a support tool, not a substitute for physician diagnosis.
